# Card

A card serves as a container for information and actions related to a specific concept or object. Cards combine images, text, and interactive elements in a rectangular box to enhance visibility of information and facilitate predictable browsing patterns.

## Selectable

## Expandable

The expandable type replaces the two-button footer with a single 40×40 chevron button aligned to the end of the card. Use `expanded` and `onExpand` to control the toggle state.
