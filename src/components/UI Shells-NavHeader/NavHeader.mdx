## RTL Support

RTL is implemented with physical CSS mirroring — no `dir="rtl"` wrapper is placed on the header or its children.

- **Menu items RTL** — `flex-direction: row-reverse` on the item renders `[chevron][label]` visually (chevron on the left).
- **Actions RTL** — `flex-direction: row-reverse` renders `[label][icon]` visually (icon on the right).
- **Logo+menu container RTL** — `justify-content: flex-end` keeps both logo and menu items right-aligned. DOM order swaps (menu items first in DOM) so both sit at the right edge.
- **Tablet RTL** — utility side on the left, nav-menu button on the right.
- **Mobile RTL** — utility (hamburger) on the left, nav-menu (3 dots) on the right.
