**Half Donut geometry (Figma-matched):**

| Property | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| viewBox | `0 0 280 145` | Height = CY + 5px padding |
| Center (CX, CY) | `140, 140` | Arc flat bottom at y=140 |
| Outer radius | `130` | Matches Figma 320×160 frame proportions |
| Inner radius | `85` | Ring thickness = 45px |
| Arc span | −180° → 0° | Left (9 o'clock) → Right (3 o'clock) via top |
| Inner hole | Upper-semicircle `<path>` | Full circle would overflow below the SVG and cover the legend |
| Center label y | `90` | 35% into inner hole (y=55…140) |
| Center value y | `117` | 71% into inner hole |

**Legend behaviour rules:**

- Clicking a legend item hides/shows the corresponding arc and recalculates percentages.
- At least one segment is always visible (clicking the last visible item has no effect).
- Hidden segments are shown with `--chart-legend-hidden` (#d2d6db) dot color and 45% opacity.
- `showLegend={false}` hides the legend entirely (useful for compact half-donut cards).
- Legend does not appear in `empty` or `loading` states regardless of the `showLegend` prop.
