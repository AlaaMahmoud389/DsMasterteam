<h2 id="usage" style={{ fontSize: 20, fontWeight: 700, color: '#000b36', marginBottom: 8 }}>Usage Guidelines</h2>

<div style={{ display: 'grid', gridTemplateColumns: '1fr 1fr', gap: 20, marginBottom: 28 }}>
  <div style={{ border: '1.5px solid #d1fae5', borderRadius: 8, padding: '16px 18px', background: '#f0fdf4' }}>
    <div style={{ fontSize: 13, fontWeight: 700, color: '#065f46', marginBottom: 10 }}>Do</div>
    <ul style={{ margin: 0, paddingLeft: 18, fontSize: 13, color: '#374151', lineHeight: 1.8 }}>
      <li>Use for onboarding, wizards, or checkout flows with a clear step sequence</li>
      <li>Use <code>md–xl</code> sizes for the primary stepper; <code>xs–sm</code> for compact indicators</li>
      <li>Use <code>theme="dark"</code> on dark-colored section backgrounds</li>
      <li>Use the neutral variant when the primary blue clashes with surrounding content</li>
      <li>Always provide both <code>onBack</code> and <code>onNext</code> when the flow supports bidirectional navigation</li>
      <li>Test with screen readers — verify the <code>aria-label</code> updates when step changes</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div style={{ border: '1.5px solid #fee2e2', borderRadius: 8, padding: '16px 18px', background: '#fff5f5' }}>
    <div style={{ fontSize: 13, fontWeight: 700, color: '#991b1b', marginBottom: 10 }}>Don't</div>
    <ul style={{ margin: 0, paddingLeft: 18, fontSize: 13, color: '#374151', lineHeight: 1.8 }}>
      <li>Don't use for more than 8–10 steps — arc segments become indistinguishable</li>
      <li>Don't rely on colour alone to communicate progress — the counter text is required</li>
      <li>Don't use on dark backgrounds without <code>theme="dark"</code> — track and text will be invisible</li>
      <li>Don't place multiple large steppers (<code>xl</code>) on the same view</li>
      <li>Don't use for non-linear or optional flows — use a tab or breadcrumb instead</li>
      <li>Don't omit the <code>label</code> prop when users may not know which step they are on</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>
